About these terms

These terms apply when you use this website to learn about The Prestige Signature Standard Academy, request a discovery conversation, or register for training. A confirmed training engagement is the registration Prestige accepts after payment is received.

Inquiries

A discovery inquiry is a request for Prestige to contact you. It does not reserve a date or confirm a program. Prestige follows up about the training you describe.

Private training

Private programs serve cohorts of up to 25 participants. Choose the program, the available training dates, and either a 50% deposit or payment in full. A private request stays pending until Prestige confirms that payment was received and reviews the agreement reference.

Open Enrollment

Open Enrollment training availability begins November 2, 2026. Individuals choose their program and an available date or training block. The first confirmed registration establishes that program’s cohort for those dates.

Each cohort has a maximum of 25 participants. Registration closes at capacity, 48 hours before the session, or when Prestige closes the date.

Program investment

Approved private training starts at $3,750 for Half-Day, $6,000 for Full-Day, $10,000 for Two-Day and $35,000 for the Five-Day Full Academy. Open Enrollment is $300, $500, $900 and $3,200 per person respectively. All prices are in USD.

Payment and confirmation

Review your program, dates, and payment selection before you pay. A request holds a place for 16 hours. Payment does not by itself confirm registration. Prestige confirms your place after payment is received.

For a question about your training agreement, a cancellation, a new date, or a refund, contact Prestige. The Academy will discuss the request with you.

The credential

The Prestige Signature Standard Professional Service Credential™ is earned only by meeting the published Five-Day Full Academy requirements, including attendance, knowledge, practical assessment, and the Module 15 capstone. A certificate of completion is not that credential. The credential is valid for two years.

Names and materials

The Prestige Signature Standard™ and the P.O.I.S.E. Method™ name the Academy’s standard and teaching sequence. Text, photographs, and training descriptions on this site belong to the Academy and are here so you can learn about the programs. They are not a license to copy the curriculum or present it as someone else’s program.

Using the site

Use the site to read about the Academy and to request contact or registration. Do not disrupt the site, or submit another person’s information without permission to do so.

Changes to these terms

Prestige may update this page. The date above is the current version.
